RollerSkater is a fast, offline-first Android music player crafted with Jetpack Compose, Material 3, and Kotlin Coroutines/Flow. It is built from the ground up to respect your privacy, your device resources, and your music library.

🔒 100% OFFLINE & PRIVACY-FIRST
• Zero tracking, zero telemetry, zero crash uploaders, zero third-party ads.
• Strictly on-device: all listening history, playlists, favorites, and metadata never leave your phone.
• No internet permission required for playback: complete privacy by architectural design.

🎵 AUDIOPHILE-GRADE PLAYBACK ENGINE
• Powered by AndroidX Media3 / ExoPlayer with custom DualPlayerEngine.
• Extended codec support via native FFmpeg decoding: FLAC, ALAC, Opus, APE, WMA, AC-3, E-AC-3, DTS, and more.
• 32-bit float PCM direct decoding for pristine bit-perfect audio reproduction.
• True gapless audio playback and smooth configurable audio crossfade.
• Sleep timer with smooth volume ramp-down.

🎨 MATERIAL 3 EXPRESSIVE DESIGN
• Dynamic Material You color schemes matched to your wallpaper or album artwork.
• Pure AMOLED dark mode for deep contrast and battery savings on OLED screens.
• Predictive back gesture support across all player sheets, queue lists, and dialogs.
• Full immersive edge-to-edge layout honoring system cutouts and navigation bars.
• Smooth, hardware-accelerated rounded corners and fluid transitions.

📝 SYNCHRONIZED LYRICS
• Real-time synchronized lyrics rendering with line-by-line and word-by-word tracking.
• Support for embedded audio lyrics, external LRC files, and TTML formats.
• Offline caching ensures your lyrics are always available without network requests.

🏷️ METADATA & TAG EDITOR
• High-performance audio tagging powered by native TagLib bindings.
• Edit song title, artist, album, genre, track number, disc number, and release year directly on your device.
• Batch album art management and cache optimization.

📊 PRIVATE ON-DEVICE LISTENING STATS
• Detailed local listening insights: top tracks, favorite artists, most played albums, and listening hours.
• Annual listening recap story generator computed 100% locally on your device.
• Never shared, scrobbled, or uploaded to any remote service.

📱 HOME SCREEN WIDGETS
• Modern AndroidX Glance AppWidgets for quick playback control.
• Responsive layouts that adapt seamlessly to any home screen grid size.

💾 ATOMIC BACKUP & RESTORE
• Export your playlists, preferences, and listening stats into portable, verifiable JSON backups.
• Complete data sovereignty: move your library metadata between devices effortlessly.
